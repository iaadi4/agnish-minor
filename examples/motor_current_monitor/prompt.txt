12 V DC-powered Wi-Fi controller for a small brushed DC motor (up to 0.5 A) and one 5 V relay output, with an INA226 high-side current and power monitor on the motor supply read by the MCU over I2C. Reverse-polarity, overcurrent and surge protection on the input, screw terminals for the motor and the relay contacts, a UART programming header, a status LED and four M3 mounting holes on an 80 x 60 mm board.
